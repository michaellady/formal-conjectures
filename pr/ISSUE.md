**Title:** Sturmfels–Sullivant conjecture on phylogenetic invariants (disproved 2026; ℤ/4 open)

**Statement.** For a finite abelian group G and every n, the ideal of phylogenetic invariants of
the general group-based model on the claw tree K_{1,n} is generated in degree at most |G|.

**Status.** False in general: G. Pochuev (Sept 2026) gave a counterexample for Z/17, n = 3
(https://github.com/Grisha-Pochuev/phylogenetic-complexity-z17-counterexample), acknowledged at
https://www.math.uni-konstanz.de/~michalek/conjectures.html. Proved for Z/2, Z/3 and Z/2 x Z/2.
The smallest group for which the bound is not known is Z/4.

**References.**
- Sturmfels, Sullivant, "Toric ideals of phylogenetic invariants", J. Comput. Biol. 12 (2005),
  no. 2, 204-228, Conjecture 29 (Conjecture 27 in https://arxiv.org/abs/q-bio/0402015).
- Michałek, https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.04177, Section 2: the combinatorial restatement I plan to
  formalise. Proves the case Z/3.
- Michałek–Ventura, https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.02584 (Z/2 x Z/2), https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.07263
  (finiteness).

I plan to work on this: the negation as a solved statement, Z/4 as the open variant.
